Understanding Rasburicase and G6PD Deficiency : Rasburicase is a widely used medication in the prevention and treatment of tumor lysis syndrome (TLS). However, it is critical to consider potential contraindications when administering Rasburicase, particularly in patients with glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency. Rasburicase is a recombinant urate oxidase enzyme that facilitates the breakdown of uric acid into an inactive compound, allantoin.
