A need for rapid G6PD assessment G6PD deficiency is the most common enzyme deficiency in humans, affecting millions worldwide. G6PD-deficient individuals may suffer life-threating complications from hemolysis triggered by certain foods, drugs, and infections. G6PD-deficiency in newborns can lead to high bilirubin levels that in some cases can progress to irreversible brain damage.
