Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency is among the most common human enzyme defects, present in about 400 million people across the globe1. Approximately 80,000 newborns are born with G6PD deficiency each year in the US alone2. G6PD deficiency is an inherited genetic disorder that affects red blood cells (RBCs)3.
